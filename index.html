Questions, answered

Everything parents ask us

How does a blanket become a book?

The artwork is printed to match the folds. Fold the blanket in half, then in half again, and again, and the front cover lands on top with the QR code on the back. Open it and pages 1 and 2 are side by side like a real book. Keep unfolding and the rest of the story appears across the whole blanket.

What is the "Scan to Watch" code?

Every BlankyBook has a QR code on its cover. Scan it with any phone and the exact story printed on the blanket plays as an animated bedtime movie. Read it first, then watch it, or the other way around.

What sizes do you make?

Two sizes: Cuddle (50 × 60 inches) for toddler beds and couch snuggles, and Big Kid (60 × 80 inches) that covers a twin bed. Both fold into the same book shape.

What ages is it for?

The stories are written for ages 2 to 8: short enough to read in about five minutes, exciting enough for a big kid, and gentle enough for lights-out. Every hero ends the story fast asleep.

Can I wash it?

Yes. Machine wash cold on gentle and tumble dry low. The artwork is printed into the fleece, not on top of it, so it stays soft and bright.

Which stories are available?

Right now: The Little Knight and the Moon Dragon, The Little Princess and the Starlight Unicorn, and our Halloween story, The Little Skeleton and the Midnight Lantern. More stories are on the way, and personalized editions with your child's name are in the works.
